Total IPTV Pro — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Total IPTV Pro is a free IPTV player for your PC. This policy explains, in plain words, what happens with your information.

Your information stays on your PC

  • Your provider login (server URL, username, password), M3U playlist URL, favorites, resume points, recordings folder choice, theme and other settings are saved only on your PC.
  • The app has no account of its own, no advertising and no analytics or tracking by Bill Foster.
  • The app does not collect, sell or share your personal information with Bill Foster.
  • Login details never leave your PC except when the app contacts the IPTV provider you typed in (or the playlist URL you pasted) so it can load your channels, movies and series.

No built-in channels or playlists

  • Total IPTV Pro does not include any TV channels, movies, series, music or other media.
  • You bring your own legal IPTV provider or M3U playlist. You are responsible for having the right to use that service.
  • The Store edition starts with a blank login screen. Nothing is pre-filled.

Playback and your provider

  • When you connect, the app talks to the server or playlist URL you entered so it can show your catalog and play streams.
  • What you watch and how that provider handles your account is between you and that provider. Providers have their own privacy policies.
  • Playback uses an external media player already on your PC (VLC recommended; mpv or ffplay also work). That player has its own privacy policy.

Optional sharper posters (TMDB)

  • If you turn on sharper posters / ratings and type your own TMDB API key (or set one in an environment variable), the app may ask themoviedb.org for artwork and ratings for titles you browse.
  • Leave the key blank and that never happens. The key stays on your PC.

Tips (optional)

  • The app is free. The optional "Send a tip" button opens PayPal in your web browser (billf195@yahoo.com).
  • PayPal handles the payment and your payment details under PayPal's own privacy policy. The app never sees your payment details.
  • A tip does not unlock anything.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. Content from your own provider may not be suitable for children; use parental controls on your PC and provider if needed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
